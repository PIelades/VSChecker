Sign in to see your shop

The app reads your shop with your Riot login cookie. You only need to do this once.


  1. Sign in with Stay signed in ticked. A "page not found" page afterwards is normal.
  2. Press F12, open Application (Storage in Firefox), then Cookies and https://auth.riotgames.com.
  3. Copy the value of the cookie named ssid and paste it here.


Keep your ssid private. It works like a password for your Riot account.
